MyHealth Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

MyHealth is a personal health and fitness application designed for private, individual use. It allows its user to collect, view, and analyze health, fitness, recovery, sleep, and activity information from supported data sources.

WHOOP Integration

MyHealth may connect to a WHOOP account through WHOOP's official authorization system (OAuth).

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, MyHealth may access WHOOP data including:

  • Recovery information
  • Physiological cycle information
  • Sleep information
  • Workout and activity information
  • Basic profile information, when authorized
  • Body measurements, when authorized

How Data Is Used

Data obtained from WHOOP is used only to provide health and fitness functionality within MyHealth, including displaying, organizing, comparing, and analyzing the user's own health and activity data.

MyHealth does not sell WHOOP data or use it for advertising.

Data Sharing

MyHealth does not sell or disclose WHOOP data to advertisers or data brokers.

Data may only be transferred to another service when explicitly requested or authorized by the user as part of an application feature.

Data Storage

MyHealth is designed primarily as a personal application. Health and fitness information may be stored on the user's device as necessary to provide the application's features.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored sensitive information and authentication credentials.

Authentication

Access to WHOOP data requires authorization through WHOOP. MyHealth does not require the user to provide their WHOOP password directly to the application.

Authorization can be revoked through the mechanisms provided by WHOOP.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as necessary to provide the application's functionality and in accordance with applicable requirements of the connected data provider.

The user may disconnect their WHOOP account and may remove locally stored application data.

Children's Privacy

MyHealth is not intended for use by children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of MyHealth or its integrations changes. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
